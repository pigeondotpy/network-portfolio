03 · Engineering method

How I approach a network problem

Production troubleshooting is rarely one command. I work from evidence, reduce the problem space, validate the theory and make the smallest safe change.

01
ObserveTelemetry, logs, routing state and user impact.
02
IsolateReduce the fault domain and identify what changed.
03
ValidateTest assumptions against protocol and platform behaviour.
04
PlanDefine the smallest change, rollback and success criteria.
05
ChangeExecute deliberately with production risk in mind.
06
VerifyConfirm convergence, service health and documentation.
